Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

WaveLink is a local-first HiFi music player. This policy explains what information we handle and how.

1What We Collect

We do not collect any personal data. There is no account system, no login, no analytics, and no advertising SDK in WaveLink. We do not track you or your device.

2Information You Provide to Enable Features

  • NAS / WebDAV connection: When you configure a NAS or WebDAV server, the server address and your credentials are stored only on your device (or in your system's secure keychain) and are used solely to connect to the servers you specify.
  • Apple Music import: With your permission, WaveLink accesses your Apple Music library only to import or read music you select. This permission is used only for that purpose.

3How Data Is Used and Stored

All data you provide is used exclusively to make the app features you requested work. Your NAS/WebDAV credentials never leave your device and are never transmitted to us or any third party. Music metadata stays local or on the servers you configure.

4Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with any third party.

5Data Security

Credentials are stored securely in your device's keychain where available. Communication with NAS/WebDAV servers may be encrypted depending on the protocol you choose (e.g., HTTPS).

6Your Control and Deletion

You can remove any NAS/WebDAV configuration at any time within the app, or by uninstalling WaveLink, which removes all locally stored data. You can revoke Apple Music access at any time in your device settings.

7Children's Privacy

WaveLink is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

8Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "last updated" date above.

9Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at: owen.lee.dev@outlook.com
